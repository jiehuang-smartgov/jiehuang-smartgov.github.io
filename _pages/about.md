I am a Lecturer at the **School of Smart Governance, Renmin University of China**. My research examines how public-sector data and intelligent systems can support collaboration, responsible decision-making, and public value. Drawing on information resource management, I focus on **public data governance**, **semantic interoperability**, **smart city data use and evaluation**, **data governance for AI applications**, and **memory governance for LLM agents**.

I received my Ph.D. and M.A. in Information Resource Management from Renmin University of China, and my B.A. in Archival Science from Nankai University. I was also a CSC-sponsored visiting scholar at the Center for Technology in Government, University at Albany, SUNY.

Since December 2022, I have been a registered expert of **ISO/IEC JTC 1/WG 11 on Smart Cities**. My contributions to international standardization projects on data use in smart cities have included serving as a project secretary and editor and helping organize international workshops. I also participate in related **ITU-T** standardization activities.

## 中文简介

我是中国人民大学智慧治理学院讲师，主要研究公共数据治理与跨部门协同、语义互操作与标准化治理、智慧城市数据利用与评估、面向人工智能应用的数据治理，以及大模型智能体记忆治理。我的研究立足于信息资源管理，结合人工智能、术语、知识组织、元数据和文件档案管理等视角，关注公共数据与智能系统如何支持协同行动、负责任的决策和公共价值创造。

自2022年12月起，我作为ISO/IEC JTC 1/WG 11智慧城市工作组注册专家参与国际标准化工作，承担相关标准项目的秘书、编辑及国际研讨活动组织等工作，并参与ITU-T相关标准化活动。
