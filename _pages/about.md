I am a Lecturer at the School of Smart Governance, Renmin University of China. My research focuses on **smart governance**, **public data governance**, **data standardization**, **semantic interoperability**, and **AI data governance**.

I received my Ph.D. and M.A. in Information Resource Management from Renmin University of China, and my B.A. in Archival Science from Nankai University. I was also a CSC-sponsored visiting scholar at the Center for Technology in Government, University at Albany, SUNY.

My work connects information resource management, public-sector data governance, smart city data use, and standardization governance. I participate in international standardization activities in ISO/IEC JTC1 WG11 and ITU-T, with work related to data use in smart cities, metaverse terminology, AI-native systems, and smart governance.

中文简介：我是中国人民大学智慧治理学院讲师，主要关注智慧治理、公共数据治理、数据标准化、语义互操作与人工智能数据治理。我的研究结合信息资源管理、智慧城市数据利用、标准化治理和政府数据治理实践。

## Research Areas

- Smart governance and public data governance
- Data use in smart cities
- Data standardization and semantic interoperability
- AI data governance
- Government information resource management and records/document management

## Recent Highlights

- Lecturer, School of Smart Governance, Renmin University of China.
- Principal investigator of the RUC “Biaoyuan” project on cross-department public data semantic interoperability and collaborative governance.
- Registered expert of ISO/IEC JTC1 WG11 and ITU-T, contributing to international standardization projects.
- Author/co-author of publications in information resource management, data governance, standardization, and smart city data use.
