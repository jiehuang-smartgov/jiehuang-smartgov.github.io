## Standardization Work

**标准化工作**

**Registered expert, ISO/IEC JTC 1/WG 11 on Smart Cities, since December 2022.** My contributions include project secretarial and editorial work, document drafting, and helping organize international workshops.

自2022年12月起为 ISO/IEC JTC 1/WG 11 智慧城市工作组注册专家，承担相关标准项目的秘书、编辑、文稿起草及国际研讨活动组织工作。

### ISO/IEC Standards and Projects

- **ISO/IEC 25005-1:2026** and **ISO/IEC TR 25005-2:2025** — Major participant and third editor; published International Standard and Technical Report, respectively.  
  智慧城市数据利用框架、用例分析与共性考虑；主要参与人、第三编辑；分别为已发布国际标准和技术报告。

- **ISO/IEC DTS 25005-3** — Major participant and second editor; draft Technical Specification on measurement, evaluation, and reporting; under development, approval phase.  
  智慧城市数据利用测量、评估与报告；主要参与人、第二编辑；技术规范草案，在研、审批阶段。

- **ISO/IEC AWI TR 25005-4** — Participant; Technical Report project on case studies of special considerations for AI applications; under development.  
  面向人工智能应用的特殊考虑案例研究；项目参与人；技术报告项目，在研。

- **ISO/IEC AWI 26605-1** — Participant; smart city vocabulary, taxonomy, terminology, and ontology project on concepts and relationships; under development.  
  智慧城市词汇、分类法、术语与本体的概念及关系；项目参与人；在研。
