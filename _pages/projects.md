# Projects

## Principal Investigator

### Cross-department Public Data Semantic Interoperability and Collaborative Governance for Smart Governance

**Funding:** Renmin University of China “Biaoyuan” Special Project, Cultivation Discipline and New Liberal Arts Start-up Fund Project.  
**Project No.:** 26XNN021  
**Role:** Principal investigator.

This project studies semantic interoperability issues in cross-department public data governance for smart governance scenarios, and explores collaborative governance mechanisms and standardization support pathways.
