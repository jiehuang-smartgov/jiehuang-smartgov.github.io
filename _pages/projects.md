My project work focuses on public data governance, semantic interoperability across departments, smart city data use, and standardization. It also includes interdisciplinary research on digital intelligence and ethics, data governance for large models, and multimodal technologies. Selected projects and research group participation are listed below, with my role indicated for each.

我的科研项目工作主要围绕公共数据治理、跨部门语义互操作、智慧城市数据利用与标准化展开，并涉及数智与伦理交叉研究、大模型数据治理和多模态技术。以下列出主持项目、部分参与项目及研究组工作，并注明本人承担的角色。

## Principal Investigator

**主持项目**

### Cross-department Public Data Semantic Interoperability and Collaborative Governance Mechanisms for Smart Governance

**面向智慧治理的跨部门公共数据语义互操作与协同治理机制研究**

- **Funding / 资助来源:** Renmin University of China, “Biaoyuan” Special Project / 中国人民大学“标原”专项。
- **Programme / 项目类别:** 培优学科与新文科启动基金项目（年度项目培育计划）。
- **Project No. / 项目编号:** 26XNN021。
- **My role / 本人角色:** **Principal Investigator / 主持人**。

This project examines semantic interoperability in public data use across departments. It explores how departments can develop a shared understanding of data concepts and meanings, together with collaborative governance mechanisms and supporting standardization approaches for smart governance.

本项目围绕智慧治理场景中的跨部门公共数据语义互操作问题，研究不同部门对数据概念和含义的理解与衔接，并探索相应的协同治理机制及标准化支撑路径。
