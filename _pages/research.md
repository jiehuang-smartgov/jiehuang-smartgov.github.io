# Research

My research examines how public-sector data can be governed, standardized, shared, interpreted, and used responsibly in smart governance contexts. The following themes summarize my current research agenda.

## Smart Governance and Public Data Governance

This stream studies governance mechanisms for public-sector data, with attention to data sharing, public data authorization and operation, data quality, cross-department collaboration, and institutional arrangements for smart governance.

## Data Use in Smart Cities

This stream focuses on data use frameworks, use-case analysis, measurement, evaluation, and reporting in smart cities. It is closely connected to international standardization work on the ISO/IEC 25005 series.

## Semantic Interoperability and Standardization Governance

This stream examines how concepts, definitions, terminologies, metadata structures, and standardization processes support semantic interoperability across organizations, departments, systems, and governance scenarios.

## AI Data Governance

This stream explores governance frameworks for data used in artificial intelligence systems, especially in public-sector and smart city contexts. It pays attention to accountability, data quality, governance rules, and standardization pathways.

## Information Resource Management and Records/Document Management

This stream connects data governance with information resource management, terminology, metadata, and records/document management, particularly under structured data and digital governance environments.
