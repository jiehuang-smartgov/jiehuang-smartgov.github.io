My research examines how public-sector data can support collaboration, decision-making, and public value. I connect the institutional, semantic, and evaluative aspects of data use, and explore how governance arrangements need to adapt to AI applications.

Drawing on information resource management, I bring together perspectives from terminology, knowledge organization, metadata, and records and archives management. These perspectives inform my work on shared understanding, data provenance, traceability, and accountability.

我的研究关注公共部门如何通过合理的治理安排、对数据含义的一致理解，以及对数据利用的有效评价，促进跨部门协同与公共价值创造，并适应人工智能应用带来的新需求。研究立足于信息资源管理，结合术语、知识组织、元数据及文件档案管理等视角。

## 1. Public Data Governance and Cross-Department Collaboration

**公共数据治理与跨部门协同**

I study how governance rules, organizational responsibilities, and coordination mechanisms shape public data sharing and use across departments. My interests include public data sharing and authorized use, data quality, accountability, and institutional arrangements that connect data resources with public services and decision-making.

A central question is how cross-department data sharing can translate into sustained collaboration and effective data use.

这一方向关注公共数据共享与授权运营、数据质量、组织职责及协作机制，研究数据治理制度如何与具体业务和公共服务需求相衔接。

**核心问题：**跨部门数据共享如何通过清晰的职责、规则与协作机制，转化为持续的协同行动和有效的数据利用？

## 2. Semantic Interoperability and Standardization Governance

**语义互操作与标准化治理**

I examine how concepts, terminology, ontologies, and metadata support shared understanding across organizations and information systems. I also study how standardization processes contribute to the development and coordination of data governance rules.

This theme includes my work on cross-department public data semantic interoperability and collaborative governance under RUC research project **26XNN021**.

这一方向关注不同部门、系统和业务场景中的概念差异与语义衔接，研究术语、概念体系、本体和元数据如何支持共同理解，以及标准制定和协调过程如何影响数据治理实践。

**核心问题：**当不同部门对同一概念或数据项有不同理解时，如何通过知识组织、标准化与协同治理实现可持续的语义互操作？

See [Projects](/projects/) for related project information.

## 3. Smart City Data Use and Evaluation

**智慧城市数据利用与评估**

I investigate frameworks for describing, analyzing, measuring, evaluating, and reporting data use in smart cities. My work considers how data use can be assessed in relation to application contexts, governance needs, and public value.

This theme is closely connected to my participation in international standardization work on the **ISO/IEC 25005 series**, including work on data use frameworks and use-case analysis.

这一方向关注智慧城市中的数据利用过程、场景和评价，研究如何描述数据利用需求、分析用例、形成评价依据，并报告数据利用的过程与结果。

**核心问题：**如何从“数据是否可获得”进一步走向“数据是否得到有效利用”，并结合具体治理场景评价其价值？

See [Standards](/standards/) and [Publications](/publications/) for related work.

## 4. Data Governance for AI Applications

**面向人工智能应用的数据治理**

I explore how public-sector data governance can respond to the changing requirements of AI applications. My interests include data quality and suitability, traceability, accountability, and the adjustment of governance rules as application contexts evolve.

My current research also considers **agile governance approaches to data use in smart cities**, examining how data requirements, organizational responsibilities, and governance arrangements can be reviewed and adjusted throughout the development and use of AI applications.

这一方向关注人工智能应用对数据质量、适用性、可追溯性和责任界定提出的新要求，研究公共部门的数据治理如何随应用场景与数据需求的变化而调整。

**核心问题：**当人工智能应用持续改变数据需求和使用方式时，如何形成能够及时调整、同时保持责任清晰的数据治理安排？

## 5. Memory Governance for LLM Agents

**大模型智能体记忆治理**

My research interests include memory governance for large language model (LLM) agents, with particular attention to urban governance and public service applications. I connect governance requirements, such as accountability, traceability, and human oversight, with technical mechanisms for organizing, retrieving, updating, and correcting agent memories.

This direction covers three related topics:

- **Guidelines for developing and applying urban AI agents:** Examining application scenarios, functional and responsibility boundaries, memory use requirements, human intervention, and evaluation criteria for agents in urban governance.

- **Error correction in long-horizon agent tasks:** Investigating error identification, failure-trace analysis, strategy adjustment, and task recovery, including how unreliable memories can be corrected and useful lessons from failure can be retained.

- **Agent memory retrieval:** Exploring how agents organize and retrieve past experiences according to task relevance, temporal validity, provenance, and access conditions, and how conflicting or outdated memories affect subsequent actions.

A central question is how agents can draw on previous experience while reducing the inappropriate reuse of outdated information and unsuccessful strategies.

这一方向关注大模型智能体在持续交互和任务执行中形成的记忆，研究如何将责任明确、过程可追溯和人工监督等治理要求，与记忆组织、检索、更新和纠正等技术机制相结合，支持城市治理与公共服务场景中的可靠应用。

主要研究议题包括：

- **城市智能体建设及应用指南：**研究城市治理场景中的智能体建设与应用要求，关注功能与责任边界、记忆使用规则、人工介入条件及应用评价。

- **长程智能体纠错机制：**研究多步骤任务中的错误识别、失败轨迹分析、策略调整与任务恢复，探索如何修正不可靠记忆、保留有价值的失败经验，并减少错误策略的重复使用。

- **智能体记忆检索：**研究面向任务的记忆组织与检索，关注相关性、时效性、经验来源、访问条件以及冲突或过时记忆的处理，分析记忆检索对后续行动的影响。

**核心问题：**如何使智能体在长期运行与长程任务中恰当地记录、检索和修订经验，利用失败反馈进行纠错，并使记忆使用及其对行动的影响可追溯、可评价、可控制？
