# Standards

My standardization work focuses on data use in smart cities, public data governance, terminology, metaverse-related concepts, information/document management metadata, and AI-related governance scenarios.

## Overview

- Published international standards: 2
- Published national standards: 3
- Published local standards: 4
- Ongoing international standards: 3

## International Standards

| Standard | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ITU FGMV-33 *Glossary for metaverse* | Main drafter, second author | Published, June 2024 |
| ITU FGMV-21 *Principles for building concepts and definitions related to metaverse* | Main drafter, third author | Published, December 2023 |
| ISO/IEC CD 25005-1 *Information Technology - Data use in smart cities - Part 1: Framework* | Major participant, third editor | Ongoing since August 2021 |
| ISO/IEC CD TR 25005-2 *Information technology - Data use in smart cities - Part 2: Use cases analysis and common considerations* | Major participant, third editor | Ongoing since August 2021 |
| ISO/IEC AWI TS 25005-3 *Information Technology - Data use in smart cities - Part 3: Measurement, evaluation and reporting* | Major participant, second editor | Ongoing since August 2021 |
