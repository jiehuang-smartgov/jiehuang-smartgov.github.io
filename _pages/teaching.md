My teaching connects artificial intelligence and data technologies with data management, governance, and practical problem solving. I emphasize conceptual understanding, critical reflection, and responsible academic and technical practice.

我的教学围绕人工智能与数据技术、数据管理和数据治理展开，注重将基础概念、实践方法与真实场景相结合，支持学生形成清晰的问题意识、规范的学术表达和负责任的技术应用意识。

## Selected Courses

**主要课程**
### Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence and Data Technologies

**Undergraduate course · 本科课程：人工智能与数据技术基础**

An introductory course covering knowledge representation and reasoning, machine learning, neural networks, large language models, and intelligent agents. It connects core concepts with data practices and practical applications, helping students understand how AI systems work and critically assess their capabilities and limitations.

课程介绍知识表示与推理、机器学习、神经网络、大语言模型和智能体等核心内容，结合数据技术与实际应用，帮助学生理解人工智能系统的基本原理，并判断其能力、局限与适用场景。

### Data Management and Data Mining

**Undergraduate course · 本科课程：数据管理与数据挖掘**

This course connects data management across the data lifecycle with core data mining techniques. It covers data planning, collection, storage, quality, and security management, together with data cleaning and preprocessing, feature preparation, classification and regression, clustering, association rule mining, and anomaly detection. The course emphasizes selecting appropriate methods for practical problems, evaluating model performance and discovered patterns, and interpreting and communicating findings through visualization. Students develop an understanding of the process from data preparation and modeling to the application of results, and how data management decisions affect the quality and reliability of data mining.

课程将数据全生命周期管理与数据挖掘技术相结合，涵盖数据规划、采集、存储、质量与安全管理，并介绍数据清洗与预处理、特征处理、分类与回归、聚类、关联规则挖掘和异常检测等技术。课程围绕实际问题，关注挖掘方法选择、模型性能与挖掘模式的评价，以及结果可视化、解释与表达，帮助学生理解从数据准备、建模分析到结果应用的完整过程，以及数据管理决策对数据挖掘质量和可靠性的影响。

### Data Management and Data Governance

**Master's course · 硕士课程：数据管理与数据治理**

A graduate course examining data management capabilities and data governance arrangements. Topics include data architecture, integration, metadata and master data, quality, sharing, organizational responsibilities, standards, and public-sector data use. Literature discussion and case analysis connect technical practices with governance questions.

课程面向研究生讨论数据管理能力与数据治理安排，涉及数据架构、集成、元数据与主数据、质量、共享、组织职责、标准及公共部门数据利用，通过文献讨论与案例分析连接技术实践和治理问题。

## Student Research and Supervision

**学生研究与指导**

My student supervision connects smart governance with public data governance, semantic interoperability, smart city data use, and memory governance for LLM agents. I emphasize clear research questions, careful engagement with literature, appropriate methods, and transparent reporting of evidence.

我的学生指导围绕智慧治理、公共数据治理、语义互操作、智慧城市数据利用以及大模型智能体记忆治理等方向展开，重视研究问题凝练、文献分析、方法选择、证据组织和规范表达。

Topics of interest for student research include:

- **Public data governance and collaboration:** Data sharing, organizational responsibilities, and coordination across departments.

- **Semantic interoperability and standardization:** Concepts, terminology, metadata, ontologies, and the coordination of data meanings across organizations and systems.

- **Smart city data use and AI data governance:** Data use scenarios, evaluation, data quality, accountability, and governance arrangements for AI applications.

- **Memory governance for LLM agents:** Agent memory retrieval, error correction in long-horizon tasks, and guidelines for developing and applying urban AI agents.

学生研究可围绕以下议题展开：

- **公共数据治理与协同：**公共数据共享、组织职责及跨部门协作机制。

- **语义互操作与标准化：**概念、术语、元数据、本体及跨部门数据含义的协调。

- **智慧城市数据利用与人工智能数据治理：**数据利用场景与评价、数据质量、责任界定及面向人工智能应用的治理安排。

- **大模型智能体记忆治理：**智能体记忆检索、长程任务纠错机制，以及城市智能体建设及应用指南。

For the broader research agenda, see [Research](/research/).

## Teaching Innovation

**教学改进**

I am exploring how foundational AI and data courses can develop students' AI literacy by connecting conceptual understanding, practical activities, and smart governance cases. This work also considers how to evaluate students' understanding of AI capabilities, limitations, and responsible use.

我的教学改进关注数智基础课程中的人工智能核心素养培养，探索如何将概念讲授、实践活动、文献讨论与智慧治理案例相结合，并评价学生对技术能力、局限及负责任使用的理解。
